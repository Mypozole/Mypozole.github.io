What makes ours different

No shortcuts on the pot. No decisions made for you on the bowl.

Most pozole sits under a layer of grease you have to eat around. Ours doesn't — we skim it off every single batch, the way it's done at home, not in a restaurant kitchen. What lands in your bowl is broth, hominy, and meat, nothing extra.

Then it's yours. Salsa roja or verde, avocado, crunched tostadas, a squeeze of lime — added at the table, by you, in whatever amount makes it yours.

Our story

A family recipe, cooked the same way every time.

MyPozole started with one family's recipe and a pot big enough to share it. Every batch is still made the same way — skimmed clean, no shortcuts — because that's the only way we know how to make it.
