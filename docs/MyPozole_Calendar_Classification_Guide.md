# MyPozole Calendar Classification Guide

Quick reference for where to add an event. Six calendars, one simple test.

---

## The test

**Can a member of the public walk up and buy something?**

- Yes, it's open selling → **public calendar** (Farmers Markets, Food Truck Public, or Pop-up Special Events)
- No, but a specific company's staff can attend → **Food Truck Private**, shown on Find MyPozole with the company name and a "not open to the public" note
- No, and MyPozole isn't marketing the event at all → **Catering Calendar**, shown on Find MyPozole only as a blocked "Private Event" slot, no name

Venue does not matter. A school, an office, a store, a park are all irrelevant to the classification. Only access, and whether MyPozole is responsible for promoting it, matters. A vending event at a school is public. A restricted vending event at a company campus names the company. A booked private catering job names nothing.

---

## The six calendars

| Calendar | What goes here | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| **Farmers Markets** | Every weekly farmers market stop | Public schedule, Find MyPozole |
| **MyPozole Food Truck (Public)** | Truck stops where the public can walk up and order | Public schedule, Find MyPozole |
| **MyPozole Food Truck (Private)** | Vending on a restricted campus, like Illumina or Neurocrine, where only that company's staff can attend | Find MyPozole, shown with company name and a "not open to the public" note |
| **MyPozole (Pop-up Special Events)** | Any pop-up tent event, always public by definition | Public schedule, Find MyPozole |
| **In Store Demos** | Any in-store tasting or sampling event at a retail location | Store locator, "next demo" |
| **MyPozole Catering Calendar** | Fully private bookings MyPozole is not responsible for marketing, regardless of venue | Find MyPozole, shown only as a blocked "Private Event" slot with hours, no company name |

## Format reminder for every event, on any calendar

- **Title:** location or client name, kept consistent across repeat visits
- **Location field:** the real street address, entered in Google's own location field, not just typed into the title
- **Time:** accurate start and end time

This is what lets the website read the calendars automatically. Consistent formatting now means zero extra work later.
