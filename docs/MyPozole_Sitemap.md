# MyPozole Sitemap

Every page this site will have, what each one does, and how they connect. This is the structural decision made once so the build never has to rediscover it page by page.

---

## 1. The shape of it

```
HOME
│
├── CATERING (nav dropdown, no landing hub — direct to one of three)
│    ├── Home Catering
│    ├── Office Catering
│    └── Events Catering
│
├── WHERE TO BUY
│
├── MENU
│
├── ABOUT POZOLE          (secondary, footer + contextual links only)
│
├── WHOLESALE             (footer-only, buyer lane)
│
└── CONTACT
```

Six real pages plus three catering variants. Nine total. Every one of them loops back into at least one other, so nobody hits a dead end.

---

## 2. Home

**Job:** route every visitor to the one thing they came for, fast.

- **Find MyPozole**, sticky schedule rail, live from the three public calendars, scrolling out a month or two rather than stopping at one week.
- **Menu teaser**: moved up, near the top. Each item links into its own order or redirect logic (see Menu, below), not just a description.
- **Reviews strip**: live, real customer reviews. Replaces any in-house claim about what makes the product different, including the grease/fat story, which stays a conversation Jorge has in person, not a website claim. A short nutrition stat row (protein, calories, fiber, no claim language) can sit near this or near Where to Buy.
- **Catering teaser**: three buttons, Home / Office / Events, each a direct link to its own page. No fourth "general catering" option.
- **Where to buy teaser**: condensed store preview, links out to the full Where to Buy page.
- **Wholesale teaser**: the buyer stat block, links out to Wholesale.
- **Fan photo strip**: rotating real customer photos, sourced from a dedicated Drive folder Jorge or you drop images into. No rebuild needed to add new ones.
- **Social push**: Instagram front and center, Facebook and Twitter present but secondary, both confirmed active. TikTok slot ready to activate once Jorge has content flowing.
- **"Learn more about pozole and our story"**: one quiet link near the bottom, into the About Pozole page, which now also holds Jorge's personal founding story as its own section. Not a section on Home, just a door.
- **Footer**: contact info, social icons, Wholesale link, copyright.

## 3. Catering, three separate pages

No hub page. The nav's Catering item expands to show three choices, and every visitor lands directly on one of the three.

### 3a. Home Catering
- Who it's for: family dinners, home gatherings, personal drop-off.
- Drop-off order builder: quarts, protein mix, real lead times. **Confirmed**: $64 is the real catering drop-off minimum ($16/quart × 4-quart minimum). Separate and distinct from the $40 minimum on individual online orders through the Menu page, both numbers are correct, for two different products, never merge them in copy.
- Pozole Box featured here as the elevated option, the full dinner kit with toppings, salsas, and the hosting card.
- Ends with: "Bigger group? See Events." and "Ordering for the office? See Office Catering."

### 3b. Office Catering
- Who it's for: office lunches, meetings, recurring team orders.
- Same drop-off mechanics, reframed: headcount-based quoting, a note on invoicing for businesses, and the **Volume Discount Options** tier pulled from the existing Wix ordering page.
- Ends with: "Feeding your family instead? See Home Catering." and "Something bigger? See Events."

### 3c. Events Catering
- Who it's for: weddings, large private parties, fundraisers, community events, and on-site corporate vending. Confirmed differentiator: Home and Office are both drop-off, Events is the full stand, the trailer or a farmers-market-style setup, selling the whole menu or a subset of it on-site.
- Pozole Party Packages A, B, C: on-site service, tostada bar, self-serve toppings, 30-person minimum, 50% deposit, 60-day cancellation window.
- Restricted-vending private events (Illumina, Neurocrine-style) can be named here. **Worth a quick heads-up to those companies before this goes live**, since it's their name being used publicly too, not just Jorge's call to make alone. If any pushes back, pull that one name, the rest can stay.
- Notes the on-site trailer service is pending San Diego County DEHQ licensing, not presented as bookable until resolved.
- Ends with: loops back to both Home and Office Catering.

## 4. Where to Buy

- Store locator, filterable by protein and style, built only from verified data: the stores and Maps links already confirmed on the existing retail page. No hardcoded store count anywhere in the copy, since this list is meant to outgrow "six" soon.
- Each card click opens native maps, Google, Apple, or Waze depending on device, straight to the real address.
- "Ask your store to stock it" generator, captures interest for stores that don't carry it yet.
- Bag product info: formats, price, nutrition panel, heating instructions, real photos of the actual bags and pouches, description matching the pouch labeling exactly. Style names shown as plain English, White, Red, Green, not Blanco, Rojo, Verde.
- Nutrition stats (protein, calories) surfaced here too, near the product info, as plain facts, not health claims.
- Quiet link out: "Are you a grocery buyer?" into Wholesale.

## 5. Menu

Bigger change than a reorder: Menu becomes a real order hub, not just a description page. Each item leads somewhere, and stays high on the page, right after Find MyPozole, on purpose: for a lot of first-time visitors, seeing the full range is the first real impression of the brand, not just a transaction step.

- **Order, corrected**: Pozole, Pozole Burrito, Poztada, Poznachos, Pozfrito.
- At-market photography and wording says **cup**, not bowl, since that's how it's actually served there. Mention both cup sizes offered.
- **Sourcing, confirmed by Jorge**: the commissary can fulfill pickup orders of Pozole itself and its toppings only, nothing hot, nothing assembled. So Pozole gets a real "Delivery or pickup" path, $40 minimum, $5 delivery fee, per the existing online ordering setup. The Burrito, Poztada, Poznachos, and Pozfrito have no commissary path at all, they're market and trailer only.
- **Every tile is tagged honestly with its actual path**, so nobody expects delivery on something that only exists at a market. The market-only tag reads as an invitation, "Find this at the trailer," linking straight into Find MyPozole, not as a limitation.
- Links into the three Catering pages, into Where to Buy, and into Find MyPozole for market-only items.

## 6. About Pozole (secondary)

Not in the main nav. Reachable only from the footer and from contextual "learn more" links on other pages, plus the one link near the bottom of Home. This is deliberate, it's for the visitor who wants to go deep, not the one deciding what to order tonight.

- History, the three regional styles, why the ingredients matter. Blanco, Rojo, Verde used here as educational, cultural terms, but always introduced alongside their plain English names, White, Red, Green, which stay the default everywhere else on the site.
- Traditional ingredients versus the lighter, California-leaning version most current customers actually want, most of whom are new to the dish rather than cooking it from memory. Positioning stays "healthy, hearty, tasty, with real cultural roots," not a purity argument.
- Toppings explained in depth, including an accurate note that heat comes from chile flakes added separately, not from choosing a salsa color, and that the green salsa itself carries real heat from serranos while the red does not.
- **How MyPozole started**: Jorge's personal founding story lives here as its own section, not scattered across operational copy. Sourced and written from three published pieces (SDVoyager, el Restaurante, Roaming Hunger), ready to drop in. Every other page refers to "our team," this is the one place his name and story are front and center.
- Ends with links back into Menu, Where to Buy, and Catering.
